# Stale-policy challenges

The admitted V24QUAL contains 150 evaluator-only stale-policy challenges, as independently counted in `results/v24/qualification/structural_admission.json`. The candidate sees event/system times and the unchanged bitemporal policy corpus, but not expected policy IDs or challenge markers. The synthetic fixed scenario catalog covers historical, future-distractor, out-of-order, late-correction, current-version-bias, and stale-cache cases. This is a fixed policy-selection benchmark, not a general estimate of production stale-policy risk.
